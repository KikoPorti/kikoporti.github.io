Francisco
Portillo

Undergraduate researcher in Spanish Language and Literature at Universidad de Navarra, preparing for doctoral study in linguistics.

I study the cognitive plausibility of large language models as models of human language, examining their parallels and divergences with human language acquisition and processing, and exploring whether their computational representations can offer insight into how and where language is represented and processed in the brain.

01 Research

My work sits at the intersection of language acquisition, neural language processing, and the computational study of language.

01

Language Acquisition

How linguistic knowledge emerges from limited input, with particular interest in predictive processing, surprisal, frequency, and developmental change.

02

LLMs & Human Language

Whether large language models provide cognitively plausible models of language, and what systematic similarities and divergences with humans can reveal.

03

Language in the Brain

How language is represented and processed in the brain, connecting computational measures such as surprisal with EEG, MEG, and behavioral evidence.

04

Multimodal Speech

How auditory and visual information interact during speech perception, including computational approaches to audiovisual integration and the McGurk effect.

02 Selected Publications

2026

Frequency Predicts Acquisition, Surprisal Predicts Reading: A Developmental Dissociation in Spanish

Francisco Portillo · Manuscript submitted for publication

Under Review

Investigates the relationship between LLM surprisal, child-directed input frequency, and adult reading times, revealing a developmental dissociation between early word learning and real-time language comprehension.

2026

Artificial Rigidities vs. Biological Noise: A Comparative Analysis of Multisensory Integration in AV-HuBERT and Human Observers

Francisco Portillo · SSRN Preprint

Under Review Preprint

Investigates whether AV-HuBERT reproduces the McGurk effect in ways that parallel human audiovisual speech integration, focusing on auditory dominance, fusion, and perceptual variability.

2025

Imperfect Language, Artificial Intelligence, and the Human Mind: An Interdisciplinary Approach to Linguistic Errors in Native Spanish Speakers

Francisco Portillo · arXiv:2511.01615

Preprint

Examines semantic and orthographic error-correction patterns across GPT-4, Gemini, and LLaMA, comparing model behavior with native Spanish speakers.
